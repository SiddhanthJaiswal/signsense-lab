## Design Context

### Users
The creator is building a personal technical demo: enter an English sentence and inspect a rigged avatar performing generated ASL motion.

### Brand Personality
Precise, quiet, transparent. Make the sentence and the signer's hands the focus.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, restrained studio interface with warm neutrals and a muted green accent. A large uninterrupted Three.js stage; compact sentence entry and playback controls. This direction interprets the creator's personal-demo brief and can be revised.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize the visible hands, face, and upper body.
- Keep model uncertainty visible without obstructing playback.
- Provide pause, replay, slow playback, and camera reset.
- Separate sentence translation, motion representation, and avatar rendering.
